We use HOT3D public training clips, stereo imagery, camera calibration, object poses, UmeTrack hand annotations, and object models. We select continuous source episodes, resize and annotate the images, encode paired videos, and sample the hand and CAD surfaces for display. Simulated robot controls, simulated trajectories, and scores are produced by this study.
